> **Draft — review by a qualified person before relying on it.**

## Free Demo Disclaimer

**Free hackathon demo. No payments, subscriptions, or marketing emails.**

CiteCheck AI is an open prototype and contest submission developed exclusively for educational and evaluation purposes. We do not charge money, collect billing credentials, send commercial promotional emails, or store enterprise customer data under commercial contracts.
